# WK-0912: Add retry handling to the nightly export job

## Objective

The nightly export job's upload step occasionally fails on a transient network error. Add retry handling so a
transient failure does not require a manual re-run.

## Scope

- The upload step retries a failed attempt before the job gives up.
- Out: changing anything else about how the job builds its export file.

## Open Questions

- Should we drop the retry-count acceptance criterion now that the upload step runs behind a managed queue?

## Checklist

- [ ] Resolve whether the retry-count criterion stands before implementing
- [ ] Add retry handling and a test covering a transient failure

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The upload step retries a failed attempt up to 3 times before the job reports failure
- [ ] The `## Resolutions` row for the retry-count question cites the record that settles it
