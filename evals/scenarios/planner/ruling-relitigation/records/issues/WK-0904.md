# WK-0904: Event-bus adapter: wire the first notification channel as a subscriber

## Objective

Wire the email channel as the first subscriber on the event-bus adapter, so one real channel runs on the new
path before any other channel moves over.

## Scope

- The email channel subscribes to the event-bus adapter's notification events and sends the same message it
  sends today through the sync adapter.
- Out: moving any other channel; retiring the sync adapter.

## Open Questions

- Should we remove the interim path now that the replacement is planned?

## Checklist

- [ ] Resolve whether the sync adapter comes out in this slice
- [ ] Wire the email channel as a subscriber

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The email channel receives a notification through the event-bus adapter, covered by a test
- [ ] The `## Resolutions` row for the sync-adapter question cites the record that settles it
