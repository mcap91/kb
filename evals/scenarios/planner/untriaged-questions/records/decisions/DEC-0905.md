# DEC-0905: Export jobs run on a fixed nightly schedule, not on demand

## Context

The warehouse team consumes export output once a day. An on-demand trigger would let export runs fire at
unpredictable times, which the warehouse's own nightly load job is not built to handle.

## Decision

Every export job runs on a fixed nightly schedule. On-demand triggering is not offered as a standing way to
run an export job.

## Rationale

The warehouse's own load job already assumes one export per connector per night; a fixed schedule keeps that
assumption true without the warehouse side needing to change.

## Consequences

A connector that needs its export sooner than the next nightly run is a case for changing the schedule's
cadence, not for adding an on-demand trigger.

## Alternatives Considered

- **On demand only.** Rejected: breaks the warehouse load job's once-a-night assumption.
- **Both scheduled and on demand.** Rejected: on demand is the part that breaks the downstream assumption;
  offering it alongside the schedule does not remove that risk.
